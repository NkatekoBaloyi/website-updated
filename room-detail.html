ADVENTURE / THE LOST TEMPLE

The Lost Temple

  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Players: 2-6
  • Duration: 60 min
  • Price: R350

The Story

A forgotten temple. A chamber sealed for centuries. And a mystery waiting to be uncovered. Your team has stepped beyond the stone doorway — now the symbols, hidden mechanisms and scattered clues are your only guide.

Look closer, share what you discover, and piece together the temple's secrets. You have 60 minutes to solve the final puzzle and find your escape.

What to Expect

  • Physical Puzzles - Hands-on tasks and mechanisms.
  • Immersive Sound - An atmosphere that draws you in.
  • Search and Find - Look closely at every detail.
  • Teamwork Required - Bring your observation together.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled start time.
  • No outside bags, phones, or cameras are allowed inside the room.
  • Listen to your game master's safety briefing closely.
  • If you are stuck, you can ask for up to 3 clues.
