About ADVENTURE ESCAPE SOUTH AFRICA

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PUZZLES

Our Story

An escape room begins with a simple invitation: look beyond the obvious. At ADVENTURE ESCAPE SOUTH AFRICA, that invitation takes you into stories filled with clues, hidden connections and discoveries to share.

From the first glimpse of a room to the final puzzle, the experience is about curiosity and connection. A fresh pair of eyes can spot what others miss, a shared idea can turn a mystery into a moment of discovery.

Our team brings creative direction, game design, scenic building and customer experience together — the people behind the worlds your team explores.
